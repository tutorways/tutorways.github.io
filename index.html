How can I filter a table

Data 8 Tutor

You filter a table with tbl.where(column, condition). It keeps only the rows where that column meets the condition, like are.above(10) or are.equal_to('PG'). If you leave out are and just pass a value, it matches that value exactly.

What are you trying to filter? Tell me the table, the column, and what you want to keep, and I'll point you to the next step. Textbook 6.2 (Selecting Rows) lists every are option.

The surface is the experiment.

Every surface above answers from the same course material. TutorWays keeps the tutor fixed, changes one thing, and measures what moves. We compare proprietary and open-weight models, small models, and complete tutor systems the same way.

We measure
Teaching quality, cost per response, latency, and how reliably it runs where students are.
We measure with
Human ratings, LLM judges against a written rubric, pairwise comparisons, and scripted checks.
Results will include
The methodology, the harness configuration, the test questions, and the measured data.

No results are published yet. They'll appear once the evaluation design and judges are calibrated.
